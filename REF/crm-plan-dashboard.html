What to take, what to beat, what to skip.

Referral intake speed
Manual data entry from faxes vs our AI 10-second parse

Same company, poorly integrated. BD data doesn't flow to intake
Reporting flexibility
Canned reports only. Custom requires consultants
API access
Gated and expensive. Partnership agreements required
Mobile BD app
PlayMaker mobile is dated; WellSky mobile is clinician-focused

$8k/mo, 10-20% annual increases, multi-year lock
SLA visibility
Breach alerts buried 3 clicks deep
Support quality
Degraded post-consolidation. Long tier-1 queues

07 — Integrations

8 integration points, 6 BAA-signed. Full API inventory in Insurance Claims/REF/API-INVENTORY.md

Receive faxes as email. KMS-encrypted S3 storage.
BAAT1: Stedi 270/271
Auto eligibility for Medicare/Medi-Cal on intake.
BAAT1: Bedrock Claude
Extract structured data from fax PDFs. ~$0.01-0.05/referral.

Internal API. Denial risk score per referral.

Free public API. Auto-lookup provider NPI on contact creation.
BAAT1: WorkOS
Auth, SSO, MFA, roles, directory sync, audit.
BAAT2: SES outbound
Email notifications. Link only, no PHI in body.
[CONFIRM]T2: Twilio SMS
Push alerts to BD reps. BAA available. No PHI in SMS.
